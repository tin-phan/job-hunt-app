job-hunt

job-hunt is a personal tool used by one person, its owner. It is not offered to the public.

Once a day it reads the job-alert emails (LinkedIn, Indeed and Google job alerts) that arrive in the owner's own dedicated Gmail account, picks out the job details (title, company, location, posting date and link), filters and ranks them, and sends the owner a short list in the owner's own Telegram chat.

It has read-only access to that Gmail account. It never sends, changes or deletes email.
